# Muhammad

**Repair: orient. The distinctive move is that the root is a living person, which
creates a problem the other cases do not have.**

## The containers

**Container.** A tribe, as in the Abraham case. The umma is the composite.

**The Constitution of Medina is an interface specification.** It unifies the
outward interface, so the composite presents one response to outsiders, while
explicitly preserving the inward implementation, since blood money obligations
continue to run within each tribe. That is information hiding written as a treaty
in 622.

**Why this root is different.** Abraham is a constant. Muhammad is a container
whose responses are still being computed, because he is alive and can be asked
new questions. The fixpoint is well defined only while the computation runs.

**What happens when it stops.** He dies in 632 and the constant the federation was
resting on was never a constant. The Ridda wars are the recomputation, and the
succession dispute is the federation failing to agree where the root went.

The Constitution of Medina, 622, is the clearest federation document anywhere. It
binds the Muhajirun, the Ansar and named Jewish tribes into one umma, explicitly
to the exclusion of other people.

What makes it a federation rather than an absorption is that the tribes keep their
internal structure. Blood money obligations continue to run within each tribe. The
document does not dissolve the members, it unifies the interface they present
outward while leaving the implementation alone.

Which is the Persian move again, arrived at independently and in a single
generation rather than over centuries.

## The amplifier

Ibn Khaldun supplies the mechanism, and it is the one Robin reached independently.
Tribal solidarity is naturally bounded by lineage. Religion is what carries it past
that bound, because it lets people who are not kin owe each other what kin owe each
other. Asabiyya is the scarce resource in a federation, and a shared root above
manufactures more of it than kinship can supply.

The 1744 pact between Muhammad bin Saud and Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab is the
worked example, and the three Saudi states are what came out of it.

## The succession problem

Here is the cost of choosing a living person as your root.

Muhammad died in 632 and the tribes considered their allegiance to have been
personal to him, so it lapsed. The Ridda wars of 632 and 633 were Abu Bakr
establishing by force that the federation outlived its founder.

That is the structural weakness of this repair stated exactly. A root outside the
set is what makes federation possible, and a living person is only temporarily
outside the set. He dies, and the root has to be either re-established or
transferred, and transferring it means somebody inside the set now holds it.

Abraham does not have this problem, because he was already dead when they started.
A constitution does not have it either. Muhammad has it acutely, and the entire
subsequent history of the succession dispute is the federation failing to agree on
where the root went.
