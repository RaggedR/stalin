# Putin

**Repair: orient, past the point where anything federal survives.**

## The containers

**Container.** In the 1990s, an oligarch or a governor, with a genuinely
autonomous interface and a response type that could return *no*. The graph had
cycles and Yeltsin was a weak root arbitrating between them. That was a real
confederation.

**What the vertical did.** Every node's interface was rewritten so its response
type became whatever the apex required. The forward map still runs. The backward
map now returns a constant.

**And a constant carries no information.** This is the formal statement of the
whole pathology. When every response is determined by the apex rather than by the
state of the world, the mutual information between the true state and the report
goes to zero. The channel is perfectly reliable and completely useless.

That is the readout in `terror.txt`, and February 2022 is what it costs.

**Prigozhin.** A node that attempted to be a morphism *into* the apex rather than
out of it. The diagram does not admit that arrow.

Calling Russia a confederation of criminal interests gets the criminal half right
and the confederation half exactly backwards, and the backwards half is the
instructive one.

## The criminal half is well supported

Krysha, literally roof, is the Russian word for both a gang's protection and a
patron's political cover, and one word doing both jobs is not a coincidence.
Reiderstvo is a protection racket with a courthouse attached, where the security
services seize a company on manufactured charges and hand it to someone better
connected. Killing defectors abroad is the informer rule. Dawisha argues the
arrangement was built for accumulation from the St Petersburg years onward rather
than being a state that later went bad.

Galeotti puts the careful version. Russia is not run by the mafia. The state
subordinated organised crime and uses it as an instrument.

## The confederation half describes the country Putin dismantled

A confederation is sovereign units voluntarily associated with a weak centre
derived from its members. That was Russia in the 1990s. The oligarchs were
genuinely autonomous, the seven bankers brokered the 1996 election, regional
governors ran fiefs and Yeltsin arbitrated between them.

The entire project after 2000 was the negation of that. Gusinsky and Berezovsky
driven out. Khodorkovsky jailed and Yukos destroyed in 2003. Governors appointed
rather than elected from 2004. The thing was named openly as the vertical of
power.

Prigozhin settles it. The one man who behaved as though he were a sovereign
partner in a confederation was dead two months after acting on the assumption.

## The right description

A patrimonial hierarchy, in Weber's sense. No line drawn between the ruler's
household and the state, officials as personal servants rather than holders of
office, and property held conditionally on loyalty and revocable at the apex. Some
scholars reach for kormlenie, the old Muscovite practice of granting an official a
territory to feed on in place of a salary, and it fits.

Beneath the apex it does look like competing predatory clans raiding each other
and bargaining. Horizontal and criminal underneath, vertical and absolute on top.

## What it cost

A gang boss hears the truth, because the organisation is small and his people are
close, and lying to him is dangerous but so is letting him walk into a trap. A
patrimonial sovereign is surrounded by people who hold everything at his pleasure.

Becoming the ultimate gangster destroys the exact informational conditions that
made you a competent one. The reporting on Ukraine that reached the top in early
2022 was reportedly what the top wanted to hear, and the officer responsible was
detained afterwards for it.

That is the terror mechanic, in a real state, inside living memory, at a cost
still being counted. It is also the clearest argument that the readout in
terror.txt is measuring something real.

## Where the criminal model stops working

A gangster is a rational profit maximiser, and the model stops predicting in 2022.
Invading Ukraine was catastrophic for exactly the interests a racket exists to
protect. Reserves frozen, the European gas market lost permanently, the network's
own assets destroyed. No racketeer burns down his own territory.

So something other than accumulation was driving it, and the criminal account,
which explains the period to about 2014 rather well, cannot explain the thing that
mattered most.
