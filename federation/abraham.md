# Abraham

**Repair: orient. The distinctive move is that the root sits behind rather than above.**

## The containers

**Container.** A tribe. Prompts are *muster*, *contribute*, *adjudicate*.
Responses are men, goods, and a judgement.

**The cycle.** Tribe A's response to *adjudicate* depends on B's, B's on C's, and
C's on A's. The forward map composes freely and the backward map never returns.

**Why a dead ancestor terminates it.** The ancestor is a container in the diagram
whose responses are already determined and depend on nothing. Formally he is a
constant, and a constant is well founded by definition. The recursion bottoms out
in him because there is nothing left to compute.

That is the precise reason the root has to be dead, or transcendent, or written
down. It must be a value rather than an ongoing computation.

**Composition.** The genealogy is a $\triangleleft$ chain running backwards in
time, and the tribes are the leaves.

The problem with federating tribes is that no tribe will accept another tribe as
the root, because that is submission and not federation. The root has to lie
outside the set of current members.

There are two directions in which to put it. Above, which is one god. Or behind,
which is a common ancestor. Both work for the same reason, and neither requires
anybody to concede to a peer.

## Why the ancestor solves it

A segmentary system is a dependent composition. Me against my brother, my brother
and I against my cousin, the three of us against the stranger. The group that
coheres is a function of who is opposing it, so the composite re-roots itself per
conflict. There is always a root, and it is never the same one twice.

A common ancestor lets that structure close at the top. Keep widening the circle
of opposition and eventually every member of the federation is kin. Twelve tribes
from Jacob. The Arab lines through Ishmael. The outermost circle is the whole
federation, and the ancestor is what makes the outermost circle well defined.

## The membership token is costly and irreversible

Genesis 17 attaches circumcision to the covenant. Whatever else it is, it is a
membership marker that cannot be faked and cannot be undone. A federation with
cheap exit has a weak commitment problem, and this is the opposite of cheap exit.

## The honest complication

Anthropologically the genealogies are adjusted. Segmentary lineages get rewritten
to reflect the alliances that actually exist, which means the common ancestor is
frequently the effect of the federation rather than its cause. The root is
constructed afterwards to justify a composition that already happened.

That is not a debunking. It is the more interesting version, because it says the
federation invents its own root when it needs one, and puts it in the past where
nobody can contest it.

## Caveat

Noth's amphictyony hypothesis of 1930, which read the twelve tribes as a cultic
league on the Greek model, is now largely rejected. The underlying observation
that a shared cult held a tribal confederation together does not depend on it.
Joshua 24 at Shechem still reads as an assembly binding tribes to a single root.
