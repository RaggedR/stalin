Dear Neil,

The next thing to build in the stalin game is the reporting. At the moment the
commissariats tell Gosplan the truth. They should not.

The observation is this. A commissariat that lies is still a perfectly good
container morphism. The forward map takes the query and the backward map returns
a well typed response. Every law holds. Nothing anywhere in the algebra
distinguishes a true report from a false one. That is not a gap in the theory.
It is the exact statement of why central planning is hard, and it means the
whole thing can be modelled without touching a single interface. Only what sits
behind them changes.

So each commissariat gets two layers with the same type. What is actually true,
and what it says. Gosplan sees only the second one. The player is Gosplan. At
the reckoning the first layer is revealed, and the player finds out what ledger
he was really writing for five years.

The mechanic that makes it bite is the ratchet. Next year's quota is set from
this year's reported output. So producing well is punished with a higher target,
and nobody ever reports a surplus. This is the best documented pathology of the
Soviet system and it puts the player in a real bind. Reward the good report and
you have taught the sector to lie to you.

The interesting part is how the player fights back, because this is where the
combinators do actual work rather than decorative work.

Cross checking is the product. Transport knows what it really moved. Agriculture
claims what it harvested. The discrepancy between two interfaces tells you what
neither one will say alone. The information comes out of the composition. That
is the most concrete demonstration of the algebra I can think of.

Inspection is the sequential one. Spend the resource, send the plenipotentiary,
get ground truth for one sector for one year. What you learn changes what it is
useful to ask next. Historically this is Molotov and Kaganovich being sent to
Ukraine in 1932.

The choice a commissariat makes between reporting honestly, inflating and hiding
is the sum.

Then the mechanic I would build the whole thing around. Terror improves reports
and destroys information. Shoot the commissar for a bad number and every number
after it is excellent. You have made the channel say what you want, which means
it now tells you nothing, and you are planning a famine blind while your desk
fills up with fulfilled quotas.

That is not a moral claim and it should not be presented as one. It is a control
result. Put it on the screen as a number. Compute the mutual information between
the true state and the reported state and display it as a readout which collapses
as terror rises. The player watches his own knowledge being destroyed by his own
instrument, with no commentary from the game. I do not know of another game that
does this.

On determinism. Each commissariat gets a mechanical liar, which is a seeded
function from true state, quota and fear to a report. Fully reproducible, so
calibrate.ts and optimise.py still work. Then the Claude backed version inhabits
the same interface and swaps in without Gosplan noticing. That is the sharpest
statement of your point that I can make. A language model is just another
inhabitant of the interface.

The argument this puts the game into is the socialist calculation debate. Mises
and Hayek against Lange and Lerner, on whether a central body can ever assemble
the local knowledge that the price system aggregates for free. Your half of that
argument is older and came out better. Bakunin told Marx in 1872 that a
dictatorship of the proletariat would harden into a new administrative class
ruling in the name of a people it could not see, which is the same objection
sixty years early and from below rather than from the market. The model could
run both. Lange said the board iterates towards the right prices by watching
inventories. If that works in the model then it works, and we report it. Same
discipline as the ledger. The game does not moralise and it does not conclude.

Minimum version that proves the idea. One lying sector, the ratchet, one cross
check against transport, terror, and the information readout. Everything else is
more of the same.

cheers,
Robin
